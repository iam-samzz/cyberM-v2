// About

About cyberM

A platform built to simplify cybersecurity learning.

Who We Are

cyberM is a cybersecurity learning platform. We curate the best open resources, build structured vertical career roadmaps, and offer hands-on tool training for aspiring and professional security practitioners.

What We Do

We don't sell software. We sell knowledge. Our tool training programs teach you how to use industry-standard security tools effectively — from Nmap to Burp Suite, Metasploit to Wireshark.

Our Mission

Make cybersecurity education accessible, structured, and practical. Bridge the gap between free resources and paid certifications with guided, affordable learning paths.

Structured Learning

Clear vertical roadmaps. No confusion. Know exactly what to learn next.

Open Resources

We link to the best free content across the internet. No gatekeeping.

Tool Training

Learn to use real security tools with guided, practical training programs.

Community First

Built by practitioners, for practitioners. Your growth is our metric.
